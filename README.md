# akiba-finance-app
A mobile-first application, with the purpose of income, expense and savings management. It utilizes AI for chatbot integration and ML for analytics and automation through preemptive and predictive insights. 

## Motivation
Managing personal finances effectively remains a challenge for many. Akiba Finance aims to bridge this gap by providing a mobile-first platform that simplifies income and expense tracking while leveraging AI to offer personalized financial advice and predictive insights for better savings management.

## Technology Stack
* **Language:** [Kotlin](https://kotlinlang.org/)
* **UI Framework:** [Jetpack Compose](https://developer.android.com/compose)
* **Architecture:** MVVM with Clean Architecture
* **Database & Auth:** [Supabase](https://supabase.com/)
* **Dependency Injection:** [Hilt](https://developer.android.com/training/dependency-injection/hilt-android)
* **AI/ML:** Google Gemini API & TensorFlow Lite

## Key Features
* **Expense & Income Tracking:** Easily log and categorize your daily financial transactions.
* **AI Financial Assistant:** Get personalized advice and answers to your financial queries via an integrated chatbot powered by Gemini.
* **Predictive Analytics:** Visualize future spending patterns and savings growth using machine learning models.
* **Budget Management:** Set monthly limits for different categories and receive alerts before you overspend.
* **Secure Data Sync:** Keep your financial data safe and synchronized across devices using Supabase.
* **Automated Insights:** Receive proactive suggestions on how to optimize your savings based on your spending habits.
